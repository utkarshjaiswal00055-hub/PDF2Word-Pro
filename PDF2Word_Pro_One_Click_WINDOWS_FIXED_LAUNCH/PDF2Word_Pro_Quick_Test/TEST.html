PDF2Word Pro — Quick Test

Complete this checklist in about 5–10 minutes.

  1. Register a new account.
  2. Log in and confirm the dashboard opens.
  3. Upload sample_text.pdf.
  4. Inspect the PDF and convert it to Word.
  5. Open the DOCX and check the text.
  6. Upload sample_scanned.pdf and test OCR.
  7. Check that credits decrease after conversion.
  8. Check conversion history on Dashboard.
  9. Open Pricing and test the buttons/layout.
  10. Resize the browser and check mobile/responsive layout.
  11. Check hover, upload, loading, success and download states.

If all 11 work, the local V7 quick test is complete.
